Company Overview

Leading Textile Manufacturing Excellence Since 1995

Established in 1995, Dharshini Impex is a Coimbatore-based textile manufacturing company with deep expertise in producing premium jacquard and dobby fabrics. With over 30 years of industry experience, we have evolved into a trusted fabric supplier for leading fashion labels and home textile brands across India and emerging global markets.

Operating from a fully integrated textile facility, Dharshini Impex manages every stage of production in-house—right from yarn sourcing to weaving, quality control, and dispatch. This end-to-end infrastructure empowers us to maintain superior fabric consistency, achieve faster lead times, and offer customized fabric solutions at scale.

Backed by a strong design and development team, we specialize in trend-driven textile innovation, delivering high-quality jacquard and dobby fabrics for ethnic wear, menswear, womenswear, kidswear, and home textiles. Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and timely delivery makes us a preferred choice among garment manufacturers, fashion brands, and B2B buyers.

Key Strengths & Impact

  • Stronger Customer Relationships: Trusted by leading Indian fashion and apparel brands through consistent fabric quality and dependable service.
  • Enhanced Brand Reputation: Known for innovation in design, timely fulfillment, and exceptional manufacturing reliability.
  • Faster Market Responsiveness: Agile in fabric sampling, development, and bulk production to meet ever-changing fashion trends.
  • Operational Stability: Robust, vertically integrated operations that ensure seamless execution and total quality control.
  • Increased Export Readiness: World-class infrastructure and fabric standards aligned with global B2B requirements and direct export potential.
