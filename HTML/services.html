Our Core Departments at Dharshini Impex

At Dharshini Impex, we operate through a well-structured set of departments, each contributing to the transformation of raw yarn into premium jacquard and dobby fabrics.

Designing

We have a dedicated design team that creates over 100 jacquard patterns every day. By staying ahead of fashion and textile trends, we ensure our fabric designs are both original and market-ready.

Sampling

We develop custom samples based on client needs and seasonal concepts. This department helps our buyers visualize the final product before we begin bulk production.

Product Development

We turn ideas into reality. Our product development team works closely with designing and manufacturing to test new weaves, colors, and finishes, ensuring every fabric is production-ready and trend-aligned.

Production Planning

We plan every stage of production with precision. From loom scheduling to workflow management, our team ensures efficiency and on-time delivery for every order.

Raw Material Planning

We carefully source and manage all raw materials, including yarns and dyes. Our team ensures uninterrupted supply and consistent quality to support high-volume manufacturing.

Manufacturing

We operate advanced machines like Vamatex Rapiers, Sulzer Projectiles, and Toyota Airjets powered by Stäubli Jacquards. With these technologies, we craft high-quality jacquard and dobby fabrics that meet global standards.

Quality Check

We believe quality is non-negotiable. Our team conducts thorough inspections at every step to ensure defect-free output and client satisfaction.

Packing & Inspection

We pack every order with care. Our inspection and packing team ensures all fabrics are neatly folded, labeled, and prepared for safe delivery across India and abroad.

Marketing & Sales

We build strong client relationships through our marketing and sales efforts. By understanding customer needs and industry trends, we drive growth in domestic and export markets.

Accounts & Finance

We manage finances with transparency and control. Our finance team handles billing, payroll, compliance, and supports smooth business operations across all departments.
