Technology We Use

At Dharshini Impex, we leverage cutting-edge weaving technologies to ensure every meter of fabric meets the highest standards of quality, durability, and design. Our machinery selection is purpose-driven—each system plays a vital role in shaping the character of our premium jacquard and dobby fabrics.

Vamatex Rapier Looms

Used for: Their versatility and precision, these looms are ideal for producing intricate jacquard and dobby patterns in cotton and viscose.

Impact: Enables us to create highly detailed, textured fabrics used in ethnic wear, formalwear, and contemporary fashion.

Sulzer Projectile Looms

Used for: Their ability to handle heavier yarns and wide-width fabrics, these looms produce strong and structured textiles.

Impact: Perfect for manufacturing durable greige bed linen fabrics and heavy-duty apparel fabrics with superior consistency.

Toyota Airjet Looms

Used for: Speed and efficiency, these looms are excellent for weaving lightweight, flowy fabrics.

Impact: Allows us to produce smooth, breathable fabrics used in viscose apparel and fashion-forward garments, supporting faster lead times.

Stäubli Jacquard Systems

Used for: High-speed digital control for complex jacquard patterns across thousands of warp threads.

Impact: Powers our in-house design team to create over 100 new designs daily, enabling us to produce up to 2 lakh meters/month of exclusive jacquard fabrics with clarity and consistency.
