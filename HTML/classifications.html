Welcome to Dharshini Impex

Premium Jacquard & Dobby Fabric Manufacturers

At Dharshini Impex, we design and manufacture a wide variety of jacquard and dobby fabrics tailored for apparel and home textiles. Our advanced weaving capabilities and design expertise allow us to meet evolving fashion and furnishing needs with consistent quality and creativity.

Apparel Fabrics

We specialize in fabrics for ethnic, formal, and contemporary wear, offering superior drape, texture, and finish.

1. Dobby Weaves

Our dobby weaves combine structured geometric patterns with exceptional comfort and durability. These fabrics feature small, repetitive motifs created through dobby loom technology, offering versatility for both traditional and contemporary fashion applications.

Ethnic Wear Viscose Dobby

We create soft, elegant viscose dobby fabrics ideal for ethnic wear like kurtis, salwars, and dupattas. These fabrics offer excellent drape and comfort while maintaining traditional aesthetic appeal.

Formal Wear Cotton Dobby

We produce structured and breathable cotton dobby fabrics perfect for shirts and semi-formal apparel. These fabrics combine professional appearance with all-day comfort and easy maintenance.

2. Jacquard Weaves

Our jacquard fabrics showcase intricate patterns woven directly into the fabric structure. Using advanced jacquard technology, we create complex designs with exceptional detail and dimensional texture for premium fashion applications.

a) Viscose-Based Jacquards
Satin Jacquards

We develop rich satin jacquard fabrics that offer a smooth, glossy finish ideal for premium fashion. These luxurious fabrics provide elegant drape and sophisticated appearance for special occasion wear.

Plain Self Jacquards

We weave subtle, textured self-designs that bring understated elegance to apparel. These tone-on-tone fabrics offer sophisticated texture without overwhelming patterns, perfect for contemporary fashion.

Butta (Motif) Jacquards

We offer ornamental butta jacquards with intricate woven motifs for traditional and festive wear. These decorative fabrics feature beautiful cultural patterns that celebrate heritage craftsmanship.

Two-Tone Cotton Viscose Fabrics

We engineer dual-tone fabrics blending cotton and viscose for a rich, dimensional look. These innovative fabrics combine the best properties of both fibers for enhanced comfort and visual appeal.

Pattern-Dyed Viscose Poly Fabrics

We create patterned viscose-poly blends with a soft hand feel and vibrant design detailing. These versatile fabrics offer excellent color retention and comfortable wear for everyday fashion.

Textured Self Jacquards

We specialize in creating depth and feel with textured jacquard designs for a modern twist. These contemporary fabrics add tactile interest while maintaining sophisticated aesthetic appeal.

b) Cotton-Based Jacquards
Self Jacquards

We craft woven designs using single-color threads for a tone-on-tone texture effect. These elegant fabrics provide subtle sophistication perfect for both casual and formal cotton garments.

Butta Jacquards

We produce cotton jacquards with motif placement ideal for festive and ethnic fashion. These traditional designs celebrate cultural heritage while offering modern comfort and durability.

Pattern-Dyed Poly Cotton Fabrics

We blend poly and cotton to deliver easy-care fabrics with detailed patterning. These practical fabrics combine wrinkle resistance with beautiful design elements for everyday wear.

3D Effect Jacquards

We manufacture fabrics that give a raised, tactile 3D effect for unique surface aesthetics. These innovative designs create visual depth and textural interest that sets garments apart.

Slub Cotton Jacquards

We create rustic, textured cotton jacquards with intentional yarn slubs for a handloom-inspired feel. These artisanal-style fabrics bring natural charm and authentic texture to modern fashion.

Linen-Look Jacquards

We simulate the look of linen with durable, soft cotton-based jacquards. These fabrics offer the aesthetic appeal of linen with improved care properties and consistent quality.

Craft Butta Jacquards

We design fine craft-inspired motifs woven seamlessly into the fabric. These artistic designs showcase traditional craftsmanship techniques in contemporary textile applications.

Brazzo Effect Jacquards

We innovate textures and sheens that replicate the luxurious Brazzo finish. These premium fabrics offer sophisticated surface effects for high-end fashion applications.

Double Cloth Fabrics

We engineer reversible, double-layered jacquards that offer two design surfaces in one. These versatile fabrics provide excellent value and styling flexibility for creative fashion design.

Mock Leno Fabrics

We imitate the airy leno weave using jacquard looms for strength and breathability. These fabrics combine the open structure of leno with the design capabilities of jacquard weaving.

Cotton Flax Jacquards

We incorporate flax blends for eco-friendly fabrics with natural luster. These sustainable fabrics appeal to environmentally conscious consumers while maintaining excellent performance.

Full Textured Jacquards

We offer richly textured jacquards that enhance both visual and tactile experience. These premium fabrics create multi-dimensional surfaces that engage multiple senses for exceptional fashion appeal.

Home Textiles

Our home textile fabrics bring luxury and comfort to living spaces. We create premium fabrics for bed linens, curtains, upholstery, and decorative applications, combining durability with aesthetic appeal for modern homes.

Bed Linen Jacquards

We manufacture premium bed linen fabrics with intricate jacquard patterns for luxury bedding. These fabrics offer comfort, durability, and elegant designs that transform bedroom aesthetics.

Furnishing Jacquards

We create sophisticated furnishing fabrics for upholstery and drapery applications. These heavy-duty jacquards combine aesthetic beauty with the strength required for home furnishing use.
