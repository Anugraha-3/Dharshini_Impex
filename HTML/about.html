About Us

Crafting Excellence in Textile Manufacturing

At Dharshini Impex, we combine traditional textile craftsmanship with state-of-the-art technology to manufacture high-quality jacquard and dobby fabrics. With a robust in-house production ecosystem, we manage every step — from yarn selection to weaving and dispatch — ensuring unmatched quality and consistency.

Our dynamic design team creates over 100 unique fabric designs daily, fueling a monthly production capacity of over 2 lakh meters of jacquard fabrics. This synergy of innovation and infrastructure enables us to meet diverse demands across ethnic wear, fashion apparel, and home textiles, both in India and globally.

Our Mission

To be a performance-driven textile partner delivering:

  • Exceptional Fabric Quality: Producing over 2 lakh meters of premium jacquard fabrics monthly, maintaining the highest standards of durability, finish, and aesthetics.
  • Continuous Innovation: Adapting to evolving fashion trends, technologies, and customer needs with creativity and agility.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Building long-term relationships through reliable service, timely delivery, and customized fabric solutions.
  • Sustainable Practices: Promoting eco-conscious production by reducing waste and embracing environmentally responsible methods.
  • Team Empowerment: Investing in skill-building and development to nurture a motivated, future-ready workforce.
  • Competitive Pricing: Offering high-quality fabrics at cost-effective rates to provide exceptional value for customers.

Our Vision

Leading the Future of Textile Excellence

To become a global leader in jacquard fabric manufacturing, consistently exceeding customer expectations through innovative designs, uncompromised quality, and sustainable practices — all while fostering a culture of excellence, creativity, and responsibility.

Our Values

  • Integrity in Every Thread: Honesty, transparency, and ethics in every interaction.
  • Innovation as a Habit: Staying ahead with fresh designs and evolving techniques.
  • Customer-Centricity: Custom solutions, dependable timelines, and long-term partnerships.
  • Sustainability First: Committed to responsible sourcing and eco-friendly processes.
  • Quality Without Compromise: Every meter of fabric reflects our promise of excellence.

Our Culture

We thrive on collaboration, creativity, and accountability. At Dharshini Impex, every team member is encouraged to contribute ideas, take ownership of outcomes, and continuously learn. Our inclusive and progressive workplace drives shared success — for the team, the clients, and the environment.

Our Goal

To scale production capabilities to 10 lakh meters per month of jacquard and dobby fabrics across both greige and finished categories, and emerge as a top-tier global fabric exporter from India.
